## 🌟 Section Architecture Verification

### 1. Home 1 (`index.html`) — **7 Sections**
1. **Section 1 – Hero / Main Banner**: Big bold headline, floating badges, animated stats, parallax-tilt visual.
2. **Section 2 – Our Bestsellers**: Asymmetrical product card grid, individual prices, ratings, and instant add buttons.
3. **Section 3 – Why Choose Our Chicken**: Feature cards with pop animations (Fresh poultry, shatter crust, 11 spices, express takeaway).
4. **Section 4 – Signature Chicken Collections**: Two large visual collection cards with clip-path image reveal.
5. **Section 5 – Special Deals & Combos**: 3 deal packages with discount pills and itemized lists.
6. **Section 6 – Customer Reviews**: Smooth testimonial carousel with author badges and rating stars.
7. **Section 7 – Final Order CTA**: High-impact banner with order action buttons.

### 2. Home 2 (`home-2.html`) — **7 Sections**
1. **Section 1 – Creative Split Hero**: Split-screen with bold text and modern diagonal-cut chicken showcase.
2. **Section 2 – Explore Our Menu**: 5 interactive category cards with unique visuals.
3. **Section 3 – Our Secret Crispy Recipe**: 3-step sequential storytelling of the 24h buttermilk brine & double dredging.
4. **Section 4 – Most Loved Combos**: Layered combo cards with meal details and order action.
5. **Section 5 – Our Food Experience**: Micro-batch frying, ethical poultry, and vented takeaway box science.
6. **Section 6 – Food Gallery**: Interactive image gallery with responsive popup lightbox viewer.
7. **Section 7 – Order Your Favourite**: Full-width dark promotional section with zoom & fade entrance.

### 3. Menu (`menu.html`) — **5 Sections**
1. **Section 1 – Menu Hero**: Hero banner with breadcrumbs and fast category scroll navigation.
2. **Section 2 – Chicken Buckets**: 4 distinct bucket options with unique photography and pricing.
3. **Section 3 – Wings & Strips**: 4 distinct wings & buttermilk tenders options with custom tags.
4. **Section 4 – Chicken Burgers & Combos**: 4 mouth-watering burgers and box meals.
5. **Section 5 – Sides & Drinks**: 4 sides (Cajun fries, loaded cheese fries, coleslaw, drinks) + Final Order CTA.

### 4. Offers (`offers.html`) — **5 Sections**
1. **Section 1 – Offers Hero**: Promotional banner with discount visuals.
2. **Section 2 – Today's Special Offers**: Daily deals (Lunch Crunch Box, Wing Frenzy, Twin Burger Night).
3. **Section 3 – Family Bucket Deals**: Large-format cards for big group meals.
4. **Section 4 – Combo & Weekend Specials**: Weekend-specific value deals.
5. **Section 5 – Limited-Time Promotion CTA**: Ghost pepper honey batch announcement with counter pickup CTA.

### 5. About Us (`about.html`) — **5 Sections**
1. **Section 1 – About Hero**: Brand identity introduction.
2. **Section 2 – Our Story**: Founder journey, timeline statistics (2020, 500k+ buckets).
3. **Section 3 – Our Quality Promise**: 4 pillars (100% whole cuts, pure peanut oil, small batches, eco-packs).
4. **Section 4 – What Makes Us Special**: Sensory crunch science, portion sizes, zero wait time.
5. **Section 5 – Visit & Taste**: Final visit call to action.

### 6. Contact (`contact.html`) — **5 Sections**
1. **Section 1 – Contact Hero**: Friendly food banner.
2. **Section 2 – Contact Information**: Address, phone hotlines, emails, opening hours.
3. **Section 3 – Send Us A Message**: Complete form with validation for name, email, phone, subject, and message.
4. **Section 4 – Find Our Location**: Responsive Google Maps embed with floating store badge.
5. **Section 5 – Order & Visit CTA**: Quick action banner for online orders and driving directions.

## 🎨 Theme & Accessibility Features

- **Dark / Light Mode**: Real-time switcher with `localStorage` persistence and automatic OS color-scheme detection.
- **RTL Support**: One-click layout inversion for right-to-left languages without control overlap.
- **No Console Errors**: Pure vanilla ES6+ with zero dependency bloat.
- **Responsive Breakpoints**: Seamlessly tested across 360px, 390px, 768px, 1024px, 1280px, and 1920px.
- **Respects Motion**: Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
